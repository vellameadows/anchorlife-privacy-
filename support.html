Help with the app, answers to common questions, and how to reach us.

Getting Started

When you first open AnchorLife, you'll set your Faith Focus, main goal, movement preference, and nutrition focus — this personalizes your Daily Anchor check-ins, Training Plan, and Nutrition suggestions. You can update any of these later by revisiting onboarding.

Each day, complete a morning check-in on the Anchor tab to receive a devotion and your daily plan. In the evening, a second check-in appears so you can reflect on how the day went.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my information private?
Yes. Everything you enter into AnchorLife — journal entries, check-ins, prayers, meal logs — is stored only on your device. Nothing is sent to us or any third party. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Do I need to create an account?
No. AnchorLife doesn't use accounts or sign-ins of any kind. Your information stays local to your device.

How do I delete my data?
Since everything is stored locally, deleting the app from your device permanently removes all of your data.

I switched phones — where did my data go?
Because data is stored only on-device and not backed up to a cloud account, information doesn't currently transfer automatically between devices. Back up sensitive entries manually if this matters to you before switching phones.

What are the Premium features?
Meal Plan, Recipes, and Premium Training are upcoming paid features currently in development. They're visible in the app as a preview of what's coming.

I found a bug or have a suggestion.
We'd love to hear it — reach out using the contact info below with as much detail as you can share (what screen, what you expected, what happened instead).
