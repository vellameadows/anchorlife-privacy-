Information You Provide

AnchorLife stores the following information, all entered directly by you within the App:

  • Your first name (optional, entered during setup)
  • Fitness, nutrition, and faith preferences selected during onboarding
  • Health-related information you choose to provide, including pregnancy trimester or postpartum stage and physical focus goals, used only to tailor general workout suggestions displayed within the App
  • Daily and evening check-in responses, including physical and spiritual reflections, gratitude entries, prayers, and journal notes
  • Meal descriptions you choose to log
  • Challenges you accept and your progress on them

Sensitive Information

Some information you may choose to enter — including pregnancy or postpartum status, physical and spiritual reflections, and journal content — may be considered sensitive personal information under certain state and federal laws. We treat this information with particular care:

  • This information is entered voluntarily and is never required to use the core features of the App.
  • It is used solely to personalize content displayed to you within the App, such as general movement suggestions.
  • It is stored exclusively on your device, as described below, and is never transmitted to us, sold, or shared with any third party for advertising, marketing, or any other purpose.
  • We do not use this information to make any healthcare, insurance, employment, or other consequential decision about you, because we never receive it in the first place.

How Information Is Stored

All information you enter into AnchorLife is stored locally on your device only, using Apple's on-device storage systems. As of the date of this policy, AnchorLife does not use a remote server, cloud database, external account system, or any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking service. We do not have access to your journal entries, check-ins, health information, or any other content you enter, and nothing you type into the App is transmitted to us or to any third party.

If this changes in a future version of the App — for example, if we add optional cloud backup, account sign-in, analytics, or in-app purchase processing — we will update this policy before that change takes effect and, where required by law, seek your consent.

In-App Purchases

If AnchorLife offers subscriptions or in-app purchases, those transactions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Apple's handling of payment information is governed by Apple's own privacy policy, available at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Data Security

Because your data stays on your device rather than passing through our systems, the App's security depends significantly on your device's own security (such as your device passcode and iOS security features). We recommend keeping your device's operating system up to date and using a passcode or biometric lock. No method of storage is 100% secure, and while we do not operate servers that could be breached, you are responsible for securing physical and digital access to your own device.

Your Rights and Choices

Because we do not collect, receive, or store your information ourselves, there is no external copy of your data for us to access, correct, or delete on your behalf — you have direct control over it at all times through the App. You may:

  • Edit or delete any journal entry, check-in, or log directly within the App
  • Delete all App data at once by deleting the App from your device

Residents of California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, and other states with comprehensive privacy laws may have additional statutory rights regarding personal information. Because AnchorLife does not collect your information, most such rights are inherently already satisfied — there is no data held by us to access, correct, delete, or opt out of selling (we do not sell data). If you have questions about your rights, contact us using the information below.
