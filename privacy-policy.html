Privacy Policy for AnchorLife

Last updated: [insert date]

AnchorLife ("the App") is a personal faith and wellness app. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

Information We Collect

AnchorLife stores the following information, all entered directly by you:

  • Your first name (optional, entered during setup)
  • Fitness, nutrition, and faith preferences you select during onboarding
  • Daily and evening check-in responses, including physical and spiritual reflections, gratitude entries, prayers, and journal notes
  • Meal descriptions you choose to log
  • Challenges you accept and your progress on them

How This Information Is Stored

All information you enter into AnchorLife is stored locally on your device only. AnchorLife does not use a server, cloud database, or any external account system. We do not have access to your journal entries, check-ins, or any other content you enter. Nothing you type into the App is transmitted to us or to any third party.

Third-Party Services

AnchorLife does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. No data is sold, shared, or transferred to outside companies.

Data Deletion

Because all data lives on your device, you can delete it at any time by deleting the App, which permanently removes all locally stored content.

Children's Privacy

AnchorLife is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
